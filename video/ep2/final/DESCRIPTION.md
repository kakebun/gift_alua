# What If You Woke Up in Ancient Rome?

One evening you find a glowing hourglass in your grandpa's attic. You flip it… and wake up on a street in Ancient Rome, 100 AD. No money, no idea how to get home, and a merchant yelling at you in Latin.

In this POV History episode, Max shows you what everyday life in the biggest city of the ancient world was really like: seven-storey apartment blocks with no running water, chamber pots emptied out of windows, street food drowned in fermented fish sauce, public baths, toilets with no walls, gladiators, chariot races and fires that broke out all the time.

🏛 Fun facts from this video
• Rome had around a million people at its peak.
• Upper floors of an insula were the cheapest, and the most dangerous.
• Roman law let you sue if something was thrown on you from a window.
• Carts were mostly banned from the streets in daytime, so deliveries came at night.
• Garum (fermented fish sauce) was the Roman ketchup.
• Romans used a sponge on a stick (tersorium) instead of toilet paper.
• Gladiators were expensive to train and didn't always fight to the death.
• The Circus Maximus could hold around 150,000 spectators.
